Privacy Policy for Yashora

Effective Date: August 20, 2026 | Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Yashora is an AI-powered reel and video creation application developed and operated by Ritvyom / Suryadev Nishad.

Yashora allows users to create AI-generated scripts, voiceovers, images, videos, subtitles, reels, and other digital content. The application also provides optional Google Sign-In, cloud project synchronization, community sharing, third-party AI services, and direct sharing to other applications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Yashora collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and what choices users have. By using Yashora, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Company Notice: Yashora is currently operated by an individual developer and does not represent a separately registered company unless otherwise stated.

2. Information Yashora May Process

Yashora may process different types of information depending on the features you use. This may include:

  • Google account information used for authentication
  • Firebase user ID & basic profile information
  • AI prompts, topics, scripts, and voiceover text
  • Generated images, videos, subtitles, and audio files
  • User-created projects and user-selected media
  • Community-shared content and cloud-synchronized projects
  • Device and technical information
  • Advertising and analytics information, where applicable

Yashora does not require users to manually enter an email address or password when using Google Sign-In.

3. Google Sign-In

Yashora provides a Google Sign-In / Google One-Tap authentication option. Google may provide information necessary to authenticate your account (Name, Email address, Google account identifier, Profile image). Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. Yashora does not receive or store your Google account password.

4. Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication manages user accounts and stores Firebase User ID (UID), Email address, provider info, and sign-in timestamps. This data is used solely to authenticate, maintain accounts, secure the app, and allow account deletion.

5. Local Storage on Your Device

Many Yashora features operate locally. Projects, drafts, generated assets, app preferences, user-provided API keys, and temporary rendering files remain on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to Yashora's servers merely because they exist locally.

6. User API Keys

Yashora may allow users to provide their own third-party API keys (e.g., ElevenLabs). User-provided API keys remain stored locally on the device and are never uploaded or synced to Yashora's backend, Firestore, or cloud databases. Keys are transmitted only directly to the respective third-party provider API to fulfill user requests.

7. AI Script & Content Generation

Prompts, ideas, and script requirements entered by the user are transmitted to the applicable AI provider to generate creative output (scripts, scene descriptions, captions). Users should avoid entering sensitive personal data into AI prompts.

8. AI Voice Generation

Text required for voiceovers is sent directly to voice generation services (like ElevenLabs). Generated audio files are stored locally on your device for project rendering.

9. Images and Videos

User-selected local media remains entirely on the device unless the user explicitly utilizes Cloud Sync or Community Sharing features.

10. Third-Party Media Providers

Yashora may interface with media libraries such as Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash, and GIPHY for asset searches. Search keywords may be transmitted to perform queries under their respective terms and policies.

11. Cloud Project Sync & Firestore

When users opt into Cloud Sync, project metadata, scripts, and scene configurations are stored in Google Firebase Firestore to allow multi-device restoration.

12. Community Sharing

Content intentionally shared to the Yashora Community becomes visible to other users. Do not share sensitive, private, or unauthorized copyrighted materials.

13. Third-Party App Sharing (Instagram, YouTube)

Exported media shared to third-party platforms utilizes standard Android system sharing intents. Yashora does not manage data once handed over to external applications.

14. Video Export

Video rendering (including 1080p rendering) occurs locally on the device. Exported output files are saved to local device storage.

15. Device Permissions

Permissions requested (Photos/Videos, Audio, Microphone, Storage, Internet) are strictly utilized to execute user-requested actions within the app.

16. Advertising, Analytics & Crash Reporting

Yashora may integrate Google AdMob and crash reporting tools to serve ads and monitor stability. These providers collect standard identifiers (Advertising ID, IP, crash logs). Yashora does not sell personal data.

17. Account Deletion & Data Retention

Users can initiate account deletion directly inside the app. Server-side deletion removes Firestore project data and Firebase authentication credentials. Local device files can be cleared via app settings or uninstallation.
